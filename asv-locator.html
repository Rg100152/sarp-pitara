Emergency Protocol

  1. Immobilise the bitten limb like a fracture; remove rings/tight items.
  2. Keep the patient calm and lying down. Do not let them walk.
  3. Call 108 and transport to the nearest ASV facility.
  4. Perform 20WBCT on arrival; start ASV if indicated.
  5. Monitor for anaphylaxis during infusion.
